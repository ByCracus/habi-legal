The service

You must be at least 13 and meet any higher applicable age or parental-authorization requirements. Contact: polfm3@hotmail.com.

Habi Pro and payments

Habi Pro is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment, shows the price, including any taxes, before you confirm, and sends the receipt; its own terms also apply to the purchase. Habi Pro can also be activated with a code we give out; the app shows how long it lasts.

A subscription renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions; Pro then stays on until the end of the period already paid. Uninstalling Habi or deleting your Habi account does not cancel it. If a free trial is offered, its length is shown before you confirm; cancel before it ends and you are not charged. If the price changes, Google Play tells you before the new price applies.

A subscription belongs to the Google account that bought it and works on devices that use that Google Play account. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policies and the consumer rights that apply to you. When Pro ends, nothing you recorded is deleted: Pro features stop and the free plan's limits apply again, so habits over a limit are kept, but new ones can only be added once there is room.
