Habi | Account Deletion

Request deletion of your Habi account and associated data, with or without the app.

Last updated: 2026-10-06

Request account deletion

Delete in Habi

Open your profile while signed in with a verified account, choose Delete account and confirm. A recent sign-in or reauthentication may be required. Keep the app online until it confirms completion.

If your email is unverified, you cannot sign in, or deletion fails, use the email route below. Do not assume the account has been removed after an error.

Request deletion without the app

Email polfm3@hotmail.com with the subject Habi account deletion. Preferably write from the email associated with the account and say that you want the Habi account and its associated data deleted. You do not need to reinstall Habi.

We may request proportionate ownership verification before deleting data. Never send your password or verification codes. We will confirm the outcome or explain any issue, following applicable statutory deadlines, normally one month for GDPR requests with any lawful extension explained.

Data removed

Deletion removes the authentication account, private and public profile, username and friend-code reservations, synced habits, logs, notes, workouts, routines, break days, exercise-library items, settings and owned social/sync records. In-app deletion also clears the relevant account history from that device. Deletion also covers checklists, steps and activities imported from Health Connect, and redeemed Pro codes. Nothing is removed from Health Connect itself, because Habi never writes there.

Required cloud cleanup must succeed before the app removes the authentication account. If cleanup fails, retry or contact support. Guest history and other accounts' separate local stores are not deleted by deleting this account.

Limited data that may remain

A minimal authentication-identifier/deletion-flag record is retained to block old devices from recreating deleted data. It has no email, profile or tracking history and no automatic expiry at present. Some legacy relationship identifiers may remain without access to deleted content. You can ask us to review retained identifiers.

Provider security logs and backups may persist for their applicable retention periods. Support correspondence may be retained to resolve your request and comply with law, then deleted or minimized. Copies on other devices, device backups and copies previously received by friends are not remotely erased.

Other choices

Uninstalling Habi, signing out or revoking Google sign-in is not the same as deleting the Habi account. Deleting Habi does not delete your Google account. Deleting the Habi account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel it in Google Play.

For local-only history, use the app's deletion controls or your device's app-data controls; separately review device backups. To ask about access, correction or deleting particular cloud data without deleting the whole account, contact polfm3@hotmail.com.
